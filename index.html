VLM Product Attributes

Sep 2026

Fine-tuned vision-language model on product photos

Reads a product photo and returns its type, color and material as JSON. Built on Amazon Berkeley Objects, scored on a leak-checked test split of 5,437 listings that was frozen by hash before any model saw it.

  • LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen3.5-4B lifted photo-only accuracy from 0.70 / 0.72 / 0.80 zero-shot to 0.86 / 0.79 / 0.88 (type / color / material).
  • Measured against strong baselines with paired significance tests: it beats a CLIP linear probe on color and ties it on the other two. Given the product title too, it reaches 0.92 / 0.93 / 0.92, the best color score of any method, though a TF-IDF + CLIP baseline is still better on material.
  • Returned valid JSON for every test image, even without constrained decoding.
  • Served with vLLM on one RTX 4090. A shorter prompt after fine-tuning, a merged adapter and FP8 weights took throughput on the val split from 23 to 64 images/s; FP8 costs 0.65 points on color.
  • QLoRA comparison: 29% less training memory and 27% slower training, for 0.2 to 1.1 points of accuracy.
